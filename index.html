Stephen (Steve) Sloper

I bring more than four decades of experience in labor relations, union representation, and dispute resolution. In January 2024, I retired from the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) to begin a new chapter as a neutral labor arbitrator. I’m listed with both the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service (FMCS) and the California State Mediation and Conciliation Service.

🔹 Career in Labor Relations

U.S. Postal Service

  • Casual Clerk (1976–1977)
  • Letter Carrier (1978–2000)

National Association of Letter Carriers, Branch 1111

  • Shop Steward & Chief Steward (1980–1991)
  • Field Representative & Vice President (1991–2000)
  • Represented the union in over 50 arbitrations
  • Advocated before MSPB, EEOC, and California UI Board

National Labor Relations Board (2000–2024)

  • Investigated unfair labor practice cases across private sector
  • Conducted representation elections, hearings, and authored decisions
  • Served as Grievance Chair (2011–2023) for NLRB Union
  • Selected counsel and arbitrators; supervised cases and participated in national negotiations including mediation

🔹 Arbitrator Credentials

After completing FMCS’s "Becoming a Labor Arbitrator" training—where I drafted evaluated decisions under a veteran’s supervision—I joined both the FMCS and California State arbitration panels. I’m currently available for appointments on various issues across the public and private sectors.
